ASCENSION – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026

ASCENSION is a single-player puzzle game distributed by Quest ReQuest. Contact: questrequestvr@gmail.com.

Data and accounts

The current game does not require account registration. The Android wrapper contains no advertising or developer analytics SDK and has no in-app purchase implementation.

Third-party web resources

Some game runtime modules, fonts, and styles are retrieved over the internet from services including aistudiocdn.com, cdn.tailwindcss.com, and Google Fonts. These services may process technical connection information, including IP address and request metadata, under their own privacy policies. Connectivity may be required for gameplay.

On-device data

The embedded web environment may use device-local storage and caching. Clear app storage or uninstall ASCENSION to delete locally retained app data.

Support

If you email the developer, the message and your email address are used to respond to your inquiry. Contact questrequestvr@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy will be revised if the game's data handling or third-party dependencies change.
