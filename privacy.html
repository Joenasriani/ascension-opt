ASCENSION – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026

ASCENSION is a single-player architectural puzzle game distributed by Quest ReQuest. Developer contact: questrequestvr@gmail.com.

Android game and accounts

The Android release with package name com.joenasr.ascension includes its game runtime, level content, styles, fonts and music in the app. Gameplay and the built-in reflection passages do not require an online account or an AI service. The game does not include advertising, in-app purchases, a developer analytics SDK, or a developer-operated telemetry endpoint.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the last opened level in its local WebView storage so that you can continue after closing the app. This progress is not sent to the developer. The embedded web environment may also retain local resource caches. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove locally retained progress and caches.

Android system and WebView diagnostics

The app uses Android System WebView to display its bundled game. Depending on your device, WebView provider and privacy settings, Google or your platform provider may process system diagnostics separately from the game's local progress. Google's WebView documentation describes usage statistics and crash reports for users who consent to sharing diagnostics, including technical system information and pseudonymous reporting tokens. The developer does not receive those reports through an ASCENSION telemetry service. Review your device's usage and diagnostics settings and Google's WebView reporting documentation for details.

Browser versions

The separately hosted browser game and older test builds may retrieve fonts or styles from third-party services such as Google Fonts and Tailwind CDN. Visiting those versions can send normal connection information, such as your IP address, user agent and requested resource, to the hosting or resource provider under that provider's policies. These hosted versions are distinct from the current bundled Android release.

Support and children

If you choose to email the developer, your email address and message are used to respond to your inquiry. The game does not request a child's name, email address or account registration and contains no player chat or advertising. Device and platform privacy settings still apply.

Changes and contact

This policy will be revised if the game's data handling changes. For privacy questions, email questrequestvr@gmail.com.
